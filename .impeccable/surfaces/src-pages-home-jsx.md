## Scope

Home page (Persuade) is the first surface; the same world restyles the whole app (nav, editor, dashboard, templates, pricing, preview) in Operate register.

Audience: students and job seekers in Uzbekistan, phone or laptop, writing a CV in uz/ru/en for free. Action: start a CV. Proof: the trilingual mechanism and the real templates. No invented testimonials or counts.

Build path: code-led. The user's recorded default is comp, but the OpenAI key returned credit_balance_exhausted, so no comp exists; the ink-basin card images are the quality bar.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A CV starts as one drop of ink on still water. The page is a suminagashi basin that answers the visitor; it refuses the category's split hero with tilted CV mockups.

OWN-WORLD: Indigo depth #0A1A33, ink current #1E2D4A, dilute ink #606D80, feathered gray #AEB6C2, mist #DDE2E8, paper-white water #F6F7F9. Marbled ink drawn live in canvas, never as a gradient. Jost light display in wide tracked caps and large thin headlines; Jost body. Hairline 1px rules, square 2px corners, filled indigo primary, outlined secondary. The newest thing is always the darkest ink.

STORY: The visitor sees ink moving, reads "a professional CV in minutes, in three languages", picks the CV's language as a drop of ink, types a name and starts. Below, templates and free/premium are clear.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed basin canvas behind everything. Left two-fifths: thin large headline, one-line lead, a working start form (name field, three language drops uz/ru/en as radios, filled "Start my CV" button), quiet link to templates. Right three-fifths: the marbling, where choosing a language drops a new ink ring and the section headings of that language float in it. Pointer drag combs the ink.

FORM: Medium-native fluid ink basin (dealt challenger, adopted by user), position 1 of the user's choice; seed key 3e5c8537.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: mathematical marbling (drop and tine transforms) in Canvas 2D; drag combs, click or language choice drops ink; static settled pattern under prefers-reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
